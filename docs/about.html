PlayTime

About PlayTime

PlayTime shows when popular Roblox games are busiest and quietest, hour by hour, so you can pick a good time to play.

How it works

About every 30 minutes, we check how many people are playing each game, using the player counts Roblox shows publicly. Over days and weeks, those counts add up to a picture of each game's week: which hours are packed and which are quiet. Times are shown in your own timezone.

Why use it?

  • Quiet hours are good for shorter queues, less lag, and learning a game without a crowd.
  • Busy hours are good for full servers, trading, and events.

What it does not tell you

Player counts show when people play. They do not say whether a game is good, fair, or safe for children. Parents should still check a game themselves and use Roblox's parental controls.

The fine print

PlayTime is an independent fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Roblox Corporation. Game names belong to their creators. Numbers are averages and can be off, especially for games we started tracking recently.

We never ask for a Roblox login, and there are no accounts or sign-ups here. See our Privacy Policy.
