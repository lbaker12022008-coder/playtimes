    What you can do here

    • Most played Roblox games – every game we track, ranked by how many people are in it at a typical moment.
    • Trending games – the biggest gainers and losers this week, from two weeks of player counts.
    • When is Roblox busiest? – the busiest and quietest hours across every game we watch.
    • Compare two games – put their weeks side by side and see which is less crowded.
    • Plan a session – a countdown to the next quiet or busy stretch for any game.
    • Event calendar – what the creators have announced, so you know which hours will be packed.
    • Remind me – on any game's page, tick the days you want and add them straight to your phone's calendar, each one at that day's own quiet (or busy) hour. No account, and nothing about you is stored.

    Where the numbers come from

    Roblox publishes how many people are in each game right now. We read that figure for every game on our list about every 10 minutes and keep it, which is something Roblox itself does not show you: it tells you about now, not about last Tuesday. Averaging those readings by hour and weekday gives each game's rhythm, and comparing one week with the next shows whether it is growing or shrinking.

    Averages are estimates built from samples, so treat them as a good guess rather than an official figure. The highest player counts we quote are different: those are single readings we really recorded. Nothing here says whether a game is good, fair or suitable for a child.
