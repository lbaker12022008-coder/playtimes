    Where the numbers come from

    Roblox shows how many people are in each game right now. We write that number down for every game on our list, about three times an hour, and we keep it. Roblox does not. It only ever shows you right now, never last Tuesday. Add our readings up by hour and by day and you can see each game's week. Compare one week with the next and you can see whether a game is growing or shrinking.

    An average is our best guess, worked out from the readings we took. A highest player count is different: that is one real reading we saw. Nothing here says whether a game is good, fair or safe for a child.
