Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

The short version: PlayTime does not ask for, collect, or store personal information about you.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, sign-ups, or logins. We will never ask for your Roblox username or password.
  • No forms, comments, or chat.
  • No names, emails, ages, or locations from visitors.

Children

Many visitors are kids, so we keep it that way on purpose. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you think a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Things that stay on your device

To show times in your timezone, the page reads your device's clock. That happens only in your browser and is never sent to us.

Our hosting provider

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any website host, Cloudflare automatically handles basic technical information (such as IP addresses and browser type) to deliver pages and protect the site from attacks. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Ads

This site does not show ads right now. If we add ads later (for example from Google AdSense), we will update this page first to explain what the ad provider collects and how to turn off personalized ads.

Game data

The player counts on this site are public numbers about games, not about people. We do not track individual players.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
